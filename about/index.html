About dailychaotic

The dailychaotic platform is a small, independently built shelf of web games and tools, made one at a time and released when they're ready. No fixed shape yet, just whatever's worth making.

It started with tic-tac-toe, reinvented with AI rivals and daily streaks, and will keep growing from there.
